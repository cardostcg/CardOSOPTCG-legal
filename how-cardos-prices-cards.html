CardOS TCG · Prices

How CardOS prices cards.

Know what you paid. Know what sold.

CardOS keeps what you paid next to a nightly market estimate, tagged Est. When there is no fresh price, it shows N/A instead of a guess.

Sources

Where prices come from

Market prices come from TCGplayer for One Piece, Magic: The Gathering, Gundam, Riftbound and Palworld. One Piece also gets Cardmarket (EU) prices. Digimon cards are in the catalog, and their prices are coming soon.

Cadence

How often prices update

  1. 01

    Captured nightly.

    Each game has its own scheduled price capture every night.

  2. 02

    Checked before use.

    A price that jumps past 5x or drops below a fifth of its recent median is held back. So is a high price that has not moved in days, since that is usually a listing ask, not a sale.

  3. 03

    Health checked daily.

    A daily check confirms that every price source ran and wrote a normal number of rows.

Est.

What Est. means

Est. is an estimate from the latest market price for that exact printing, parallels priced on their own. It is not a sale price or an offer. What you paid is stored as you entered it and is never filled in for you.

N/A

Why some cards show N/A

  • The set is brand new and the market has not priced most cards yet.
  • The card rarely lists, so there is no recent price.
  • The game is listed but still waiting on prices, like Digimon today.
  • The night's price failed a safety check and was held back.

In every case CardOS shows N/A. It never shows a made-up number.

FAQ

CardOS pricing FAQ

Where do CardOS card prices come from?

CardOS reads market prices from TCGplayer each night for One Piece, Magic: The Gathering, Gundam, Riftbound and Palworld. One Piece also gets nightly Cardmarket (EU) prices. Digimon cards are listed, and prices are coming soon.

How often do CardOS prices update?

Every night. Each game has its own scheduled capture, and a daily health check confirms that each price source ran.

What does Est. mean in CardOS?

Est. is an estimate from the latest market price for that printing. It is not a sale price and not an offer. What you paid stays next to it, so the two are never mixed.

Why does a card show N/A?

N/A means CardOS has no fresh price for that card. That happens with brand new sets, cards that rarely list, games still waiting on prices, or a price that failed a safety check. CardOS never fills in a guessed number.
