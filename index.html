SEC_08 // Queries

Common questions

What is the best app to track One Piece TCG profit?

CardOS TCG: Portfolio Tracker tracks One Piece, Riftbound, and Palworld cards with cost basis and profit, not just a collection total. Log any card by hand. Free core features on Android, iOS, and the web app at app.cardostcg.com. CardOS TCG: Portfolio Tracker includes CardOS OP TCG, CardOS RB TCG, and CardOS PW TCG (Palworld). CardOS GD TCG (Gundam) is coming soon.

How do I track graded One Piece TCG cards from PSA, BGS, and CGC?

Log slabs in the graded vault. Any grading company, including PSA, BGS, and CGC. Raw cards too. If a card is missing from the catalog, type it in. Unlimited logging, free.

Can I log a card that is not in the catalog?

Yes. Any card data can be tracked through manual entry: name, set, cost, grade, whatever you have.

Is there an app to track One Piece TCG profit and cost basis?

Yes. CardOS TCG: Portfolio Tracker puts a cost on each card using FIFO lots. When you sell, you get the actual gain or loss. Estimates are tagged Est. You can type your own price. Links to TCGPlayer and eBay. Core P&L is free.

What One Piece TCG apps are available on both iPhone and Android?

CardOS TCG: Portfolio Tracker is available on Android (Google Play), iOS (App Store ID 6792994816), and as a web app at app.cardostcg.com. Your collection syncs across platforms.

Is CardOS TCG: Portfolio Tracker free?

Core features are free: unlimited card logging (including manual entry), the graded vault (any grading company), current estimated value, basic P&L, price alerts, and CSV import. Pro from $3.99/month (7-day free trial), $19.99/6 months, $29.99/year, $69.99 lifetime. Pro adds advanced P&L drill-down, chart history, and CSV export. Card data is never gated. Only analysis depth is Pro.

Does CardOS TCG: Portfolio Tracker track sealed One Piece TCG products?

Yes. Booster boxes, starter decks, and case lots, with set MSRP next to current market price.

Does CardOS TCG: Portfolio Tracker track Riftbound cards?

Yes. Riftbound: League of Legends sits in the same portfolio as One Piece TCG: cost basis, realized P&L, graded vault, and sealed product tracking. RIFTBOUND: LEAGUE OF LEGENDS manufactured by UVS Games under license from Riot Games. ™ & © 2026 Riot Games, Inc.

How often are prices updated?
