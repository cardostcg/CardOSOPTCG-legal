One Piece card scanner FAQ

Is there a free One Piece card scanner app?

Yes. CardOS TCG: Portfolio Tracker has a One Piece card scanner that is free and unlimited on iOS and Android. There's no scan cap and no card needed to start.

How do I scan One Piece cards for price?

Open CardOS TCG, tap scan, and point your phone camera at the card. The app names the print and shows its market estimate, tagged Est. Tap to add it to your portfolio with what you paid.
