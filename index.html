Common questions

What is the best app to track One Piece TCG card values?

CardOS OP TCG is a portfolio tracker built specifically for One Piece TCG collector-investors. It tracks realized profit and cost basis (not just collection worth), supports raw cards and graded slabs from all 9 major grading companies (PSA, BGS, CGC, ACE, SGC, HGA, TAG, ARS, AGS), and is free to use for core features. Available on Android, iOS, and as a web app at app.cardostcg.com.

How do I track graded One Piece TCG cards from PSA, BGS, and CGC?

CardOS OP TCG has a dedicated graded card vault that supports all 9 major grading companies: PSA, BGS, CGC, ACE, SGC, HGA, TAG, ARS, and AGS, plus raw ungraded cards. You can log your slabs, track their current estimated value, and see your realized gains when you sell. The graded vault is free with no limits.

Is there an app to track One Piece TCG profit and cost basis?

CardOS OP TCG tracks realized profit and loss using FIFO cost-basis lots, the same method used in stock portfolio apps. Every card has a cost basis attached, and when you sell, the app calculates your actual realized gain or loss. Price estimates are always labeled Est., every field is editable, and each estimate links to TCGPlayer and eBay for verification. Core P&L features are free.

What One Piece TCG apps are available on both iPhone and Android?

CardOS OP TCG is available on Android (Google Play), iOS (App Store ID 6792994816), and as a web app at app.cardostcg.com. Your collection syncs across platforms.

Is CardOS OP TCG free?

CardOS OP TCG is free for core features: unlimited card logging, the full graded vault (all 9 graders), current estimated value, basic P&L, price alerts, and CSV import. A Pro tier ($3.99/month or $29.99/year) adds advanced P&L drill-down, chart history, and CSV export. Your card data is never gated. Only analysis depth is Pro.

Does CardOS track sealed One Piece TCG products?

Yes. CardOS OP TCG tracks sealed One Piece TCG products: booster boxes, starter decks, and case lots, with set MSRP next to current market price so you can see margin at a glance.
