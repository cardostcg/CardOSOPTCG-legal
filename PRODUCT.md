# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android, ios, web

## Users

One Piece, Riftbound, and Palworld TCG collectors who want to know what their collection is actually worth — cost basis, current market value, and profit/loss — across raw and graded cards. Gundam tracking is coming soon.

## Product Purpose

CardOS OP TCG is a portfolio tracker for the One Piece Trading Card Game. It logs every card (any set, raw or graded), tracks real-dollar cost basis and P/L per card, and houses a graded vault covering all nine major grading companies. The app exists so collectors can treat their collection as a real portfolio, not just a checklist.

## Positioning

Stacked, not single-axis: 9-company graded vault (PSA, BGS, CGC, ACE, SGC, HGA, TAG, ARS, AGS) + FIFO cost-basis P/L + free-forever core + radical transparency. No other One Piece TCG tracker combines all four.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Full 9-grading-company vault (PSA, BGS, CGC, ACE, SGC, HGA, TAG, ARS, AGS) plus raw.
- FIFO cost-basis profit/loss tracking per card.
- Any card, any set.
- Free core, forever — no paywall on the basics.
- Android, iOS, and web app at app.cardostcg.com.
- Static site at cardostcg.com serves as landing page, legal docs, and account management.

## Brand Commitments

- **Mark:** Icon 2.0 is the only mark. No freestyle marks, no One Piece card art in icons or thumbnails.
- **Palette:** Dark navy #0A0E1A, mint green #5DCAA5 (scarce accent, not decoration), blue CTA #378ADD.
- **Typography:** DM Sans. Tighter hierarchy; restrained sizing.
- **Hero line:** "Every card logged. Every dollar tracked."
- **Grader line (verbatim):** "PSA, BGS, and CGC graded slabs, plus raw. Full 9-company vault under the hood."
- **Free-core promise:** "Free core, forever. No paywall on the basics."
- **Support line:** "Tracks profit, not just worth."
- **Voice:** Precise, calm. No em-dashes, no hype stats, no fake urgency. Sounds like a vault, not a growth hack.
- **Imagery:** Static only.
- **Legal:** "Not affiliated with or endorsed by Bandai" where required.
- **Premium = restraint:** More navy negative space, green as scarce accent, Icon 2.0 small and sharp.

## Product Principles

1. **Portfolio, not checklist.** Every feature earns its place by helping a collector understand their position.
2. **Grading-company neutral.** All nine companies at parity; never privilege one grader's ecosystem.
3. **Free core is a promise, not a funnel.** The basics stay open. Premium adds depth, not access.
4. **Restraint is the brand.** Quiet confidence over loud persuasion — in copy, color, and layout.
5. **Radical transparency.** Show the real numbers; never obscure cost, value, or methodology.
