Questions

Before you download

Do I need an account?

No. Start in guest mode and scan right away. Make an account when you want your collection synced across iOS, Android, and the web.

Is the scanner really free?

Yes. Scanning is unlimited on the free tier. It reads cards in sleeves and names parallels.

Are there ads?

None until you've added your first card.

What does Pro add?

Paid against Est. value for each card, a 90-day portfolio trend, and CSV export. Pro Monthly is $3.99 with a 7-day free trial, Annual is $29.99, and Founder is $69.99 once for Pro for life.

Is this official?

No. CardOS TCG is an unofficial fan app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed, or sponsored by Bandai, Shueisha, or Toei Animation.
